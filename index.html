How it works

  1. Collect. Victim reports supply the first suspect wallet addresses and the amounts lost.
  2. Trace. A blockchain indexer follows outgoing transfers, including peel chains, consolidations and bridge hops.
  3. Cluster. Address-clustering heuristics group deposit addresses that share an owner, such as an exchange's hot wallets.
  4. Attribute. Clusters are matched to labelled exchange wallets, and each match gets a confidence score.
  5. Alert. New reports re-run the trace automatically, so a cash-out point is flagged while funds may still be frozen.

Limits and responsible use

A deposit at an exchange shows where funds arrived, not that the exchange did anything wrong. Most exchanges cooperate with freeze requests. Results should go to investigators and the exchange's compliance team, not be published as accusations.

Victim reports can be mistaken or malicious, so every address needs a second source before action is taken. Attribution scores are estimates and can be wrong.
